Has a golden cross worked?

We tested it. Over 771 days of Binance coins, every coin with a fresh golden cross was compared with the typical coin on the same days (the one in the middle: half the market did better, half did worse). If the cross meant nothing, it would beat the market about half the time, like a coin flip.

Golden cross (60/125)50.0%53.2%331

What that means: on its own, a golden cross has not been a reliable buy sign: after a week it did no better than chance. And a death cross was not a warning: those coins did slightly better than the market a month later. Treat a cross as one fact about a coin, not as a reason to trade. Measured 26 Sep 2026.

How Rotator counts a cross

Only completed daily candles count, so a cross can't appear and vanish within the same day. The averages are simple 60-day and 125-day closing-price averages (not the 50/200 pair some sites use), and those are the ones we tested above.
