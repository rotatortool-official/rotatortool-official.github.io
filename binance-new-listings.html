Questions

Do coins go up after a Binance listing?

On the first day, often: 24 of the 29 coins on this page closed their first day above where they opened, +42% in the middle case. After that, most fell: 30 days later 26 of 29 were below their first day's close (−31% in the middle case), and today 27 of 29 are. That is what happened before, not what happens next time.

Did they do worse than Bitcoin?

Over their first 30 days, 26 of the 29 coins did worse than Bitcoin did over the same days.

What is the Binance Seed Tag?

Binance puts the Seed Tag on new coins it sees as riskier and more volatile than others. Most coins on this page were listed with it. Users have to pass a short quiz before they can trade a Seed Tag coin.
