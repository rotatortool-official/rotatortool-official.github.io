Questions

What is the Binance Monitoring Tag?

Binance puts the Monitoring Tag on coins it sees as clearly more volatile and risky than others, and it reviews them regularly. A coin that keeps failing those reviews can be delisted. Today 31 coins carry the tag; they are listed on this page.

How much notice does Binance give before a delisting?

In the 15 spot delistings on this page, Binance announced every one 14 days before trading stopped. The announcement gives the exact time trading stops and how long withdrawals stay open.

What happens to a coin's price when Binance delists it?

Of the 15 delisted coins on this page, 14 fell between the day before the announcement and their last day of trading; the middle result was −70%. That is what happened before, not a promise of what happens next time.
