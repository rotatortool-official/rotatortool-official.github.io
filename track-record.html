Engine v2 (2026-04-26 → 2026-09-07) finished on 76.2% across 863 graded calls; its record is shown separately and is not blended into the live number — 2.1.0 changed how market cap enters the score, and 2.2.0 narrowed which coins are published at all. A call is "confirmed" when the coin beats the median coin over the 30 days following the snapshot, measured close-to-close on the same Binance daily candles for both. Half of all coins beat the median by definition, so 50% is the bar, and a figure below it means the calls were worse than picking at random. Calls that fail are split in two, because they say different things: right direction means the coin moved the way we flagged but trailed the market, and misses means it moved the wrong way outright. Until 2026-09-17 this page graded against an absolute 1.5–5% bar checked at the best moment inside a fortnight; a blanket call on every coin cleared that ~68% of the time, so it measured volatility rather than skill and was replaced. The 30-day window is where the engine’s signal is actually measurable (rank IC 0.053, t = 2.51, against 0.017 at 7 days). Not financial advice — DYOR before any trade.
