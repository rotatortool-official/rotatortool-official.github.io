Many coins don't release all their tokens at once. Early investors and the team get theirs on a schedule, and each date is an unlock: new coins that can be sold. Extra supply often weighs on the price around the date. Here is what unlocks soon for coins on Binance.

Only unlocks of at least 0.5% of a coin's supply are listed, the same line the board uses, so mining and small daily releases are left out. Schedules are refreshed several times a day. Dollar values use today's price, so they move with it.

How to read this

A large unlock, measured as a share of the coins already out there, matters more than a large dollar figure on a big coin. Not every unlocked token gets sold. About a third of the coins on the board publish a schedule. A coin missing from this page may simply have no published schedule, which is not the same as having no unlock. This is a schedule, not a forecast.
