Do coins fall before a token unlock?

In the 18 unlocks of 0.5% of supply or more since 11 Oct 2026, the middle coin moved −6.8% in the week before, and 14 of 18 did worse than Bitcoin over the same days. A small sample in a falling market, so read it as what happened, not a rule.

What happens to the price after a token unlock?

In the week after (up to 7 days so far), the middle coin moved −14%, and 15 of 18 did worse than Bitcoin, −7.5 points in the middle case. Not every unlocked token gets sold, and past results don't promise future ones.

How to read this

A large unlock, measured as a share of the coins already out there, matters more than a large dollar figure on a big coin. Not every unlocked token gets sold. About a third of the coins on the board publish a schedule. A coin missing from this page may simply have no published schedule, which is not the same as having no unlock. This is a schedule, not a forecast.
