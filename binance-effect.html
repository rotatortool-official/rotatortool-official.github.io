How it is measured

Announcements come from Binance's own announcement pages; prices are Binance daily closes in USDT. Stablecoins, gold and coins without Binance prices are left out. Rotator rebuilds every number each morning, so new listings, delistings and unlocks join on their own.

Questions

What is the Binance effect?

The idea that a Binance event moves a coin's price: a listing pushes it up, a delisting or a big unlock pushes it down. This page measures what actually happened after each of these events, from Binance's own announcements and prices.

Do coins keep their gains after a Binance listing?

Mostly not. The 29 coins Binance listed since May 2025 gained +42% on their first day in the middle case, were −31% 30 days later, and today 27 of 29 are below their first day's close (−67%).

How much do coins fall after Binance announces a delisting?

Of 15 coins Binance delisted since Jul 2026, 14 fell between the day before the announcement and their last trading day, −70% in the middle case.

Do token unlocks push the price down?

In 17 unlocks of 0.5% of supply or more since Oct 2026, the middle coin moved −6.6% in the week before and −13% in the week after, and 14 of 17 did worse than Bitcoin afterwards. A small sample in a falling market.
